Features

Your memories, quietly on display.

Sana does one thing and does it well: it shows the photos and videos you choose, all day, without fuss.

🖼️

Your albums only

Pick one or more albums. New photos you add to them show up on their own.

🎞️

Photos and videos

Full screen, never cropped. Videos play silently on loop.

🌙

Night mode

At the hours you set, the screen goes black with minimum brightness.

📍

Date and place

Optionally show when and where each photo was taken, discreetly in the corner.

🔀

Real shuffle

Every photo appears once before any repeats, so nothing gets forgotten.

🌍

9 languages

English, Português, Español, Français, Deutsch, Italiano, 日本語, 简体中文, 한국어.

How it works

Three steps. Then forget about it.

No account, no cloud, no setup wizard.

Pick your albums

Choose what the frame should show.

Set the pace

How long each photo stays, and when the night begins.

Put it on the shelf

Plug it in. Double-tap the screen any time to change settings.

The frame

Make it a real picture frame.

A wooden frame turns the iPad into something that belongs on any wall or shelf.
