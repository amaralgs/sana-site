About Sana

Sana turns an old iPad into a digital picture frame. It started from a simple observation: we take thousands of photos and almost never look at them again. A frame on the shelf that quietly shows them brings back moments, and smiles, that would otherwise stay forgotten.

The name was chosen for its calm, clean sound. The app runs on your device, and the developer has no access to your photos.
